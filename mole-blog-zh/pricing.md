## Mole CLI

Free and open source, GPL-3.0, no license key, no limits.

```
brew install mole
```

Source and issues: https://github.com/tw93/Mole

The CLI and the Mac app cover similar cleanup categories and share a protection whitelist and operations log, but the Mac app intentionally uses a narrower deletion scope. The CLI adds terminal-only commands (`mo purge`, `mo installer`, `mo touchid`); the Mac app adds the GUI, the menu bar, fan control, startup management, and in-app app updates.
