# Product name and pluralization

Last updated Apr 24, 2026|Copy as Markdown|[View as Markdown](https://developers.cloudflare.com/style-guide/formatting/product-name-and-pluralization/index.md)|[Agent setup](https://developers.cloudflare.com/agent-setup/)

Product names should follow a noun-verb agreement such that the verb is singular.

Examples:

* Cloudflare Pages enables/allows.
* Cloudflare Workers executes/propagates.
